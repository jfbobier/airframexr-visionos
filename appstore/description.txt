AirFrameXR turns Apple Vision Pro into a wireless display for PC VR games running on your own Windows PC with an NVIDIA graphics card. The game runs on your PC; this app shows the picture, tracks your head, hands and controllers, and sends them back. Everything stays on your home network.

HOW IT WORKS
• Install the free AirFrameXR streamer on your Windows PC (github.com/jfbobier/AirFrameXR), keep it running, and open this app on your Vision Pro on the same network.
• The app finds your PC on the local network, or you pick it by its address. Nothing leaves your network: no account, no cloud, no subscription.
• Games and apps that run on your PC through OpenXR are streamed with hardware video encoding, low-latency transport and foveated rendering to keep the picture sharp where you look.

FEATURES
• Low-latency wireless streaming over your local Wi-Fi (Wi-Fi 6 or newer recommended).
• Head, hand, finger and spatial-controller tracking sent to your PC; game controllers and Steam Controllers supported.
• Adjustable field of view, resolution scale and frame rate.
• Open source and built on the ALVR streaming protocol.

PRIVACY
The developer collects no data. Tracking data goes only to your own PC, on your own network.

REQUIREMENTS
Apple Vision Pro, a Windows 10/11 PC with an NVIDIA GPU (RTX 40 series or newer recommended) running the AirFrameXR streamer, both on the same local network.
